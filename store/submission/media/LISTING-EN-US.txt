PRODUCT NAME
AURIX AI

SHORT DESCRIPTION
AI creative workspace for images, writing, code and PDF questions. Internet and Google sign-in required.

DESCRIPTION
Bring your ideas into one AI creative workspace on Windows 10 and Windows 11.

AURIX AI combines image generation, writing assistance, code assistance and text-based PDF questions. Internet access, a Google account and access to third-party AI services are required. Google sign-in opens your default browser. Service availability, quotas and regional access may vary.

CREATE AND REFINE
Describe an image idea, draft or edit text, or ask for coding help. Generative AI can make mistakes: review writing, inspect image details, and test code before relying on any output.

ASK ABOUT PDF TEXT
PDF text is extracted on your device. When chat is configured and you ask a question, only the selected text excerpt used as context is sent to the chat service, not the original PDF. Scanned PDFs require OCR, which is not included.

ORGANIZE YOUR WORK
Keep local projects, folders, favorites and history. Copy or download supported outputs and optionally save supported text/code projects to your account. Download important images separately: JSON exports do not include image bytes.

KNOW THE LIMITS
- AI generation requires an internet connection; models do not run offline on your device.
- Video generation is not available.
- Image generation currently uses a square-image model; exact details and counts are not guaranteed.
- Chat depends on the operator's configured service. Provider free-tier quotas and availability apply.
- English is the primary tool language. French and Arabic/RTL interface controls are partially localized.
- Local data is separate from your regular browser profile and may be lost when the app is reset or uninstalled.
- No paid AURIX subscription or payment form is included. Unlimited or permanently free generation is not guaranteed.

Use Report AI content in the app to prepare an email report or copy a report for the developer. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it.
